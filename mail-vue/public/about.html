Aurax Mail

Send and receive real email on your own domain 用 @aurax.eu.org，收一封正经的邮件

Aurax Mail is an email service on your own domain. Create any address under @aurax.eu.org, send and receive mail with attachments and inline images — all in the browser, no client needed. Aurax Mail 是一个基于自有域名的邮箱服务。你可以用 @aurax.eu.org 下的任意前缀创建邮箱地址，收发邮件、带附件、看内嵌图片，全部在浏览器里完成，无需安装任何客户端。

What you can do 能做什么

  • Create any number of addresses on your own domain, one address per account用自有域名创建任意数量的邮箱地址，一号一址
  • Full send and receive, with attachments and inline images完整收发邮件，支持附件与正文内嵌图片
  • Manage multiple accounts in one place多账号统一管理，一个界面切换所有邮箱
  • Auto-forward mail to other inboxes or messaging tools邮件自动转发到其他邮箱或即时通讯工具
  • Auto-detect verification codes in your mail自动识别邮件里的验证码，不用再翻来翻去
  • Sign in with third-party accounts — no extra password to remember支持第三方账号登录，不必额外记一组密码

@aurax.eu.org
